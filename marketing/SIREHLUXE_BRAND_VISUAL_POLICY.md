# SirehLuxe Brand Visual Policy

Status: ACTIVE / FOUNDER-APPROVED

## Core Rule

SirehLuxe is the retail/storefront brand. It must not be presented as the manufacturer or producer of third-party or supplier products.

## Product Bottle & Packaging Rule

- DO NOT add the name `SirehLuxe`, the SirehLuxe logo, or any invented SirehLuxe manufacturer mark onto a product bottle, jar, tube, box, label, cap, or packaging unless the real source product already carries that exact branding.
- If the supplier product is unbranded, keep the product unbranded.
- If the supplier product has its own original label/branding, preserve the actual packaging faithfully unless there is a separately approved private-label arrangement.
- Never create a visual that could falsely imply SirehLuxe manufactured, formulated, bottled, certified, or produced the product.

## Where SirehLuxe Branding IS Allowed

SirehLuxe branding may appear on:

- TikTok / Instagram / Facebook cover artwork
- poster or campaign layout
- captions and CTA copy
- website / Shopify storefront
- surrounding graphic frame or brand panel
- approved watermark or end card

The branding must remain visually separate from the physical product packaging when SirehLuxe is acting only as the retailer.

## AI / Creative Production Guardrail

Before approving any generated or edited asset:

1. Compare the bottle/packaging against the verified product reference.
2. Reject the asset if `SirehLuxe` or any invented branding appears on the bottle/packaging without verified source evidence.
3. Preserve realistic bottle shape, scale, cap/dropper, label placement, and product colour where a verified reference exists.
4. Treat packaging identity as a locked product attribute unless Founder approval explicitly authorizes a private-label redesign.

## Hermes / Creative Automation Rule

Hermes and any downstream creative automation must apply this rule as a fail-closed QC gate:

`NO VERIFIED SIREHLUXE PACKAGING BRAND -> DO NOT PUT SIREHLUXE ON THE PRODUCT.`

If uncertain, keep the product packaging unchanged and place SirehLuxe branding only in the surrounding creative layout.
