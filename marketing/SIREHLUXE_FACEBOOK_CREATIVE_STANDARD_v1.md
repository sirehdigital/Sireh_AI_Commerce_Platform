# SirehLuxe Facebook Creative Standard v1

Status: ACTIVE / FOUNDER-APPROVED
Executor: MISS HERMES

## Primary Formats
- Feed master: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5) for mobile-first reach.
- Square fallback: 1080 x 1080 px when required by placement.
- Reels/Stories: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16).
- Carousel: keep a consistent aspect ratio across all cards.

## Visual Direction
- Premium, approachable beauty editorial style.
- Palette: warm cream, soft blush pink, nude, muted rose, restrained burgundy/brown typography.
- Use visual storytelling: skincare model, vanity/mirror, product use, texture, hydration, routine step, lifestyle environment.
- Default balance: approximately 70% visual / 30% text.
- Model styling must be modest; avoid revealing chest/cleavage.
- Rotate models and scene types so the page does not look repetitive.
- Keep skin realistic and naturally healthy-looking; avoid excessive retouching.

## Feed Artwork Rules
- One clear idea per creative.
- Headline: 3-8 words.
- Supporting line: maximum one short sentence.
- Put full explanation in the Facebook caption, not on the image.
- Avoid dense posters, brochure-style layouts and plain text-heavy cards.
- Product/model must remain visually dominant and readable on mobile.
- Use strong whitespace and simple hierarchy.

## Facebook Post Strategy
Balance the content mix across:
1. Education / beauty tip
2. Routine / how-to
3. Lifestyle / relatable skincare
4. Product discovery
5. FAQ / objection handling
6. Community conversation
7. Social proof only when verified
8. Soft conversion / website CTA

Facebook copy may be slightly more explanatory than Instagram, but still conversational and scannable.

## Reels Rules
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds.
- Minimal permanent on-screen copy.
- Use a custom cover when practical.
- Keep a clean reusable video master when possible.
- Do not make the Reel look like a static catalog ad from start to finish.

## SirehLuxe Product-Packaging Rule
SirehLuxe is the retail/store brand, not the manufacturer.

- NEVER place `SirehLuxe`, a SirehLuxe logo, or invented SirehLuxe branding on a bottle, jar, tube, box, label, cap, or physical package unless the verified real product already carries that exact branding.
- Supplier-unbranded packaging stays unbranded.
- Preserve original supplier branding/packaging faithfully when present.
- SirehLuxe branding may appear separately in the graphic layout, footer, page identity, caption, CTA, cover or end card.

Fail-closed:
`NO VERIFIED SIREHLUXE PACKAGING BRAND -> DO NOT PUT SIREHLUXE ON THE PRODUCT.`

## Caption Structure
1. Short hook or relatable opening.
2. 2-6 concise value lines.
3. Question, comment prompt, or soft CTA.
4. Use only a small set of relevant hashtags where useful.
5. Keep claims compliant and grounded.

## Compliance Guardrail
Preferred language:
- helps support hydration
- healthy-looking glow
- smoother-looking appearance
- lightweight feel
- simple/easy routine

Reject unsupported:
- medical/treatment claims
- DNA/regeneration claims
- guaranteed outcomes
- fabricated clinical percentages/certifications
- unrealistic before/after claims

## QC Gate
Regenerate/reject when:
- artwork is mostly text;
- creative is unrelated to beauty/skincare;
- same model/look is repeatedly reused;
- styling is too revealing;
- SirehLuxe is falsely placed on product packaging;
- packaging identity drifts from verified reference;
- copy is too small or cluttered;
- unsupported claims appear;
- asset looks generic or low quality.

## Automation Behaviour
Apply to FUTURE Facebook creative only.
Keep existing posting cadence, credentials, page connections and approved schedules unchanged.
Do not rewrite already-published posts unless Founder explicitly asks.

Founder Lock:
`PREMIUM VISUAL FIRST -> SHORT MESSAGE -> HUMAN BEAUTY CONTEXT -> VERIFIED PACKAGING -> COMPLIANT COPY -> POST`
