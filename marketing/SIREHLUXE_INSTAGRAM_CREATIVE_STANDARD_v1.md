# SirehLuxe Instagram Creative Standard v1

Status: ACTIVE / FOUNDER-APPROVED
Executor: MISS HERMES

## Primary Formats
- Feed master: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5).
- Reels: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16).
- Stories: 1080 x 1920 px (9:16).
- Carousel: keep every slide in one consistent ratio, default 4:5.

## Visual Direction
- Premium beauty editorial look: warm cream, soft blush pink, nude, muted rose, restrained burgundy/brown typography.
- Use real beauty context: skincare model, vanity/mirror, texture, dropper, product still life, hydration/water, routine moments.
- Default balance: visual first; about 70% image / 30% text.
- Model styling must be modest; avoid revealing chest/cleavage.
- Rotate models, ages, faces, hair, scenes, camera angles and backgrounds. Do not repeat the same face/look across consecutive posts.
- Preserve realistic skin texture; avoid over-retouched or synthetic-looking skin.

## Feed Artwork Rules
- One primary hook per post.
- Main headline: 3-7 words.
- Supporting copy: maximum one short sentence on artwork.
- Full education/message belongs in caption, not on the image.
- Avoid plain text-only cards unless the calendar explicitly calls for a quote/text post.
- Keep text inside mobile-safe central areas and readable at thumbnail size.
- Use whitespace deliberately and keep visual hierarchy clean.

## Reels Rules
- Hook within first 1-2 seconds.
- Keep permanent on-video text minimal.
- Custom cover required where practical.
- Cover headline must remain readable in profile-grid crop.
- Prefer clean master video without baked-in heavy copy so assets can be reused for Shopify, ads, TikTok and Stories.

## SirehLuxe Product-Packaging Rule
SirehLuxe is the retail/store brand, not the manufacturer.

- NEVER place `SirehLuxe`, a SirehLuxe logo, or invented SirehLuxe branding on a bottle, jar, tube, box, label, cap, or physical package unless the verified real product already carries that exact branding.
- If supplier packaging is unbranded, keep it unbranded.
- If supplier packaging has original branding, preserve it faithfully unless Founder explicitly approves a private-label redesign.
- SirehLuxe branding may appear separately in the creative layout, footer, watermark, cover, caption, CTA or end card.

Fail-closed:
`NO VERIFIED SIREHLUXE PACKAGING BRAND -> DO NOT PUT SIREHLUXE ON THE PRODUCT.`

## Content Rotation
1. Education / skincare tip
2. UGC / relatable routine
3. Product discovery
4. Texture / sensory
5. Lifestyle / vanity
6. Routine step
7. FAQ / myth
8. Community question
9. Soft conversion / CTA

## Caption Structure
1. Strong first-line hook.
2. 2-5 concise value lines.
3. One question or soft CTA.
4. 4-8 relevant hashtags; avoid hashtag stuffing.
5. Use compliant benefit language only.

## Compliance Guardrail
Allowed direction includes:
- helps support hydration
- healthy-looking glow
- smoother-looking appearance
- lightweight texture
- simple routine

Reject unsupported:
- medical/treatment claims
- DNA/regeneration claims
- guaranteed results
- fabricated clinical percentages/certifications
- unrealistic before/after transformation

## QC Gate
Regenerate/reject if:
- artwork is text-heavy or looks like a generic quote card;
- image is unrelated to skincare/beauty;
- same face/model is overused;
- model styling is too revealing;
- product packaging is falsely branded;
- product bottle shape/label drifts from verified reference;
- headline is unreadable in grid/mobile crop;
- unsupported claims appear;
- creative feels cluttered, cheap or inconsistent with SirehLuxe.

## Automation Behaviour
Apply to FUTURE Instagram assets only.
Preserve existing publishing cadence, credentials and approved schedules.
Do not rewrite or delete already-published content unless Founder explicitly requests it.

Founder Lock:
`PREMIUM VISUAL FIRST -> SHORT MESSAGE -> HUMAN BEAUTY CONTEXT -> VERIFIED PACKAGING -> COMPLIANT COPY -> POST`
