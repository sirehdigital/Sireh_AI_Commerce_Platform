# SirehLuxe Threads Creative Standard v1

Status: ACTIVE / FOUNDER-APPROVED
Owner: SirehLuxe Beauty
Executor: MISS HERMES

## Objective
Upgrade daily Threads visuals from text-heavy template cards into premium, scroll-stopping beauty content that still feels calm, educational and consistent with SirehLuxe.

## Core Visual Direction
- Master size: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait). Square 1080 x 1080 is allowed only when the composition genuinely works better.
- Visual balance target: approximately 70% visual / 30% text.
- Use premium beauty photography: human skincare model, real skincare action, product texture, bathroom/vanity lifestyle, or tasteful product still life.
- Palette: warm cream, soft blush pink, nude, muted rose, warm beige, restrained dark brown/burgundy typography.
- Lighting: soft natural window light or diffused beauty light. Avoid harsh contrast, busy backgrounds and generic stock-poster appearance.
- Models must be modestly styled. Avoid revealing necklines or unnecessarily exposed chest/cleavage.
- Rotate models and scene types. Do not reuse the same face/look for every post.

## Brand Placement Rule
- SirehLuxe is the STORE / RETAIL BRAND, not the product manufacturer.
- NEVER place `SirehLuxe`, its logo, or invented SirehLuxe branding on a product bottle, jar, tube, cap, box or physical packaging unless the verified real product already carries it.
- If the product is supplier-unbranded, keep the packaging unbranded.
- SirehLuxe branding may appear separately on the creative layout, headline area, watermark, footer, caption or CTA.
- Preferred visible brand text on creative: `SirehLuxe` or `SirehLuxe Beauty` only, with no leaf icon unless a separate approved asset explicitly requires it.

## Threads Post Design Rules
1. One primary message per image.
2. Main headline: 3-7 words whenever possible.
3. Supporting line: one short sentence only.
4. Do not paste the full caption into the artwork.
5. Avoid poster cards made mostly of text on an empty background.
6. Keep important text inside a generous safe zone; avoid edges.
7. The face/product should be visually dominant and immediately understandable at phone size.
8. Use whitespace deliberately; premium, not empty.
9. No fake medical, clinical, therapeutic, DNA/regeneration, guaranteed-result or before/after claims.
10. Use compliant language such as `helps support hydration`, `healthy-looking glow`, `smoother-looking appearance`, `simple routine`.

## Recommended Creative Rotation
Hermes should rotate these formats instead of repeating one template:
- Beauty Tip: model + one short educational hook.
- Routine: cleanse -> serum -> moisturize visual.
- Product Education: bottle/texture/dropper close-up with one practical tip.
- Lifestyle: woman at vanity / mirror / morning or night routine.
- Question / Conversation: strong visual + short question prompt.
- Ingredient / Texture: macro texture, dropper or water-inspired visual.
- Myth / Simplicity: `You do not need ten products` style message with human/product visual.
- Soft Product Discovery: product integrated naturally, not hard-sell advertising.

## Caption Structure
Default Threads caption format:

1. Short hook (1 line)
2. 2-4 short value lines or steps
3. One conversation question
4. Brand signature
5. 3-5 relevant hashtags maximum

Example:

`Simple skincare wins ✨`

`You do not need ten products to build a good routine.`

`Cleanse -> Serum -> Moisturize.`

`A routine that feels easy is much easier to stay consistent with. 💗`

`What is the one skincare step you never skip?`

`✦ SirehLuxe Beauty`
`Nature. Science. Radiance.`

`#SimpleSkincare #SkincareRoutine #BeautyTips #SirehLuxeBeauty`

## Quality Gate Before Posting
MISS HERMES must reject/regenerate the asset if ANY of the following is true:
- artwork is mostly plain text with little visual interest;
- same model/look has been overused recently;
- model styling is too revealing;
- SirehLuxe branding is falsely printed on the product packaging;
- bottle shape/label has drifted from a verified product reference;
- text is too small to read on mobile;
- visual is not clearly related to skincare/beauty;
- copy contains unsupported medical or guaranteed claims;
- composition looks generic, cluttered or low-quality.
