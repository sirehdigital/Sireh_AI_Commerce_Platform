   If you're building your personal-care routine from scratch, start with these three basics:

   1. Cleanse gently — once or twice a day, with a product suited to your skin, and always with light hands.
   2. Hydrate — moisturise while your skin is slightly damp, and drink enough water through the day.
   3. Stay consistent — small daily steps beat occasional effort, every time.

   Whatever your climate — humid, dry, hot or cold — let your own skin be your guide. Simple, steady, kind. ✨

   2. Hidrat — sapu pelembap semasa kulit masih sedikit lembap, dan minum air secukupnya sepanjang hari.
   3. Konsisten — langkah kecil setiap hari jauh lebih berkesan daripada usaha sekali-sekala.
