1. Tarikh: 2026-08-13 (Khamis)
2. Masa (MYT): 16:30

4. Topik: Tiga mitos biasa tentang rutin penjagaan diri — dan fakta yang lebih tenang
5. Format IG: Carousel (5 slide: cover + 3 mitos-fakta + CTA)
6. Hook/Headline: More steps. Stronger tingles. Night-only care. Three myths, gently debunked.
7. Kapsyen IG (EN):

Personal-care advice is everywhere — and some of it is just myth. Let us look at three of the most common ones:

Myth 1: "More products mean better results." The truth? Consistency with a routine that suits you matters far more than the number of steps.

Myth 2: "A product must tingle or feel intense to work." The truth? Comfort matters. The best product is the one you can use calmly, every day.

Myth 3: "Self-care only counts at night." The truth? Small caring moments all through the day — a slow morning, a midday pause — are just as meaningful.

Simple, gentle and consistent usually wins. 🌱

Mitos 1: "Lebih banyak produk bermakna hasil lebih baik." Fakta? Konsisten dengan rutin yang sesuai dengan anda jauh lebih penting daripada bilangan langkah.

Mitos 2: "Produk mesti terasa pedas atau kuat untuk berkesan." Fakta? Keselesaan itu penting. Produk terbaik ialah yang anda mampu gunakan dengan tenang, setiap hari.
